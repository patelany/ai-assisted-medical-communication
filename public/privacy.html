ClarityAI Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026

SMS Program Terms

ClarityAI sends a one-time SMS containing a secure access code to family members of hospital patients who have opted in via web form. No marketing messages are sent.

Message frequency: One message per opt-in request.

Msg & data rates may apply depending on your carrier.

To opt out: Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe immediately.

For help: Reply HELP or email clarityai@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

Mobile numbers collected through ClarityAI are used solely to deliver a one-time access code. Mobile numbers are never sold, shared, or used for marketing purposes under any circumstances.

Data Usage

ClarityAI is a research prototype. All clinical scenarios are fictional. No real patient data is collected or stored. Participant ratings are collected anonymously and exported as CSV for research analysis only.

Terms of Service

ClarityAI messages are sent only to individuals who have explicitly opted in via web form. This service is not intended for clinical use and does not constitute medical advice. Users must be 18 or older to opt in.
