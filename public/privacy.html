ClarityAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

SMS & Mobile Numbers

Mobile numbers collected through ClarityAI are used solely to deliver a one-time access code for viewing patient updates. Mobile numbers are never sold, shared, or used for marketing purposes under any circumstances.

Message Frequency

One message per session. No recurring messages are sent.

Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on your carrier.

Opt Out

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe immediately.

Data Usage

ClarityAI is a research prototype. All clinical scenarios used in the research study are fictional. No real patient data is collected or stored. Participant ratings are collected anonymously and exported as CSV for research analysis only.

Terms of Service

ClarityAI messages are sent only to individuals who have explicitly provided their phone number to the research team. This service is not intended for clinical use and does not constitute medical advice.
