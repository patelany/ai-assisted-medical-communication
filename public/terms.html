ClarityAI SMS Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026

Program Description

ClarityAI sends a one-time SMS access code to family members of hospital patients who have opted in via web form. The code is used to access plain-language medical updates at ai-assisted-medical-communication.vercel.app/view.

Message Frequency

One (1) message per opt-in request. No recurring messages.

Msg & Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

How to Opt Out

Reply STOP to any ClarityAI message to unsubscribe immediately. You will receive no further messages.

How to Get Help

Reply HELP to any ClarityAI message or email clarityai@gmail.com for assistance.

Supported Carriers

ClarityAI supports all major US carriers including AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, and Sprint. Carrier is not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Your mobile number will never be shared or sold. View our full privacy policy at ai-assisted-medical-communication.vercel.app/privacy.html
